FIELD BASICS PACK, EDITION 1 (general references, not reviewed emergency procedures)
The eight guides are original explanatory text based on the cited official references.
Seven illustrations are original AEGIS explanatory schematics drawn by the application.
Source access dates, source snapshot hashes, applicability and rights references are
included in every guide. No clinical, botanical or food-safety review is claimed.

National Park Service (NPS), U.S. Department of the Interior
Factual sources: Ten Essentials, Trip Planning Guide, Hiking Safety, NPS App Guide.
https://www.nps.gov/articles/10essentials.htm
https://www.nps.gov/subjects/healthandsafety/trip-planning-guide.htm
https://www.nps.gov/articles/hiking-safety.htm
https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/appguide.htm
Rights: https://www.nps.gov/aboutus/disclaimer.htm
No NPS logo or third-party NPS infographic is reproduced.

U.S. Geological Survey (USGS), U.S. Department of the Interior
Factual sources: What is a topographic map?; north arrows on USGS maps.
https://www.usgs.gov/faqs/what-a-topographic-map
https://www.usgs.gov/faqs/what-do-different-north-arrows-a-usgs-topographic-map-mean
The small unmodified image of US Topo north arrows is identified as PUBLIC DOMAIN
by its USGS media page: https://www.usgs.gov/media/images/north-arrows-us-topo-map
Its example angles are not current declination for the user's location.
Rights: https://www.usgs.gov/information-policies-and-instructions/copyrights-and-credits

GPS.gov / U.S. government: GPS Accuracy
https://www.gps.gov/gps-accuracy
The guide paraphrases general causes of positioning error; it does not guarantee a
specific accuracy, claim a current location fix or display actual geographic coverage.

AEGIS claims no copyright in original U.S. government works. Agency attribution does
not imply agency endorsement. Individual third-party material is not automatically
public domain. No proprietary app database has been copied into this pack.

Optional Filipino and Czech packs contain developer draft translations of the eight
general guides. Independent language/specialist review has not been performed.
Sources and original USGS image remain in their original language. App controls and
schematic labels remain English; each translated diagram has translated alt text.
